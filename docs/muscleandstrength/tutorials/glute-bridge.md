### 动作概述 (Overview)

The glute bridge against mini bands is aglute bridgevariation and an exercise used to isolate the glute muscles.The band used when performing banded glute bridges provides accommodating resistance, meaning more tension is placed on the target muscle group the closer one gets to a full contraction.The glute bridge against mini bands is an excellent exercise to incorporate into yourleg workoutsand glute workouts, or as a warm up/activation exercise prior to your workout.

### 详细动作步骤 (Step-by-Step Instructions)

1. Wrap a band just above your knees and start in a supine position on the floor with your arms at a 45 degree angle relative to your torso.
2. Bridge your hips up by squeezing your glutes and driving your heels into the floor.
3. Push out slightly on the band as you bridge up.
4. Lower your hips back to the starting position and repeat for the desired number of repetitions.

### 发力要点与教练建议 (Form Tips & Coaching Cues)

- 💡 Ensure that the lower back doesn’t arch as you extend the hip, you should focus on squeezing the glutes and slightly posteriorly tilting the pelvis.
- 💡 Ideally you should prioritize a straight line from your knees through your shoulders at the peak of contraction.
- 💡 Focus on exhaling as you bridge the hips up. You shouldn’t feel the movement through your lower back at all.
- 💡 As you push out on the band, don’t allow the knees to track out past the outside of your feet.
- 💡 If you can’t seem to feel your glutes activating, palpate the musculature with your hands and focus on pausing the movement at the peak of contraction.
